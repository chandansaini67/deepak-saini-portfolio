ABOUT

About
Deepak

Cuts, colour, storytelling. I'm Deepak Saini, a video editor who turns raw footage into short-form that people actually watch to the end. I work in Adobe Premiere Pro, After Effects and Photoshop, grading in DaVinci Resolve, with an eye for pacing and the small details that make an edit feel effortless.

  • 100M+ Views delivered
  • 50+ Clients worked with

Most of what I do is short-form: reels for Instagram creators, property films for agents, food and café content for local businesses. Every project starts the same way — find the hook, cut everything that doesn't earn its place, then grade and mix until it feels like one piece rather than a pile of clips.

I work directly with the people I edit for, so nothing gets lost in a handoff and revisions don't turn into a queue. Send me the footage and you'll get back something built to hold attention on a phone, where it's actually going to be watched.

POST-PRODUCTION

What usually goes
wrong, and how
I fix it.

Problems clients usually face

  • Poor lighting or uneven exposure in the raw footage.
  • Shaky clips, awkward framing, no visual consistency between shots.
  • Videos that feel slow, unengaging, or never quite make their point.

How I handle them

  • Correct the lighting, colour and framing so it reads clean and deliberate.
  • Strip the distractions, smooth the transitions, tighten the pacing.
  • Shape the footage into a clear, sharp story that connects with the audience.
